Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no cloud copy, so make a backup in Settings › Export backup now and then.

I got a new phone. Export a backup on the old phone, send it to yourself, then use Settings › Restore from backup on the new one. Restoring your whole iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup also brings Peony's data across.

How do I restore Peony Plus? Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel? In the iPhone Settings app: your name › Subscriptions › Peony.

Are predictions safe to use as contraception? No. They are estimates, not a method of contraception or medical advice.
