Privacy Policy

Peony: Period & Cycle Tracker · Effective 20 November 2026

The short version: everything you log in Peony stays on your iPhone. We don't have servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We never see, collect, sell or share your data.

What you log

Peony stores what you enter (periods, flow, sexual activity, temperatures, cervical mucus, ovulation and pregnancy tests, symptoms, moods, notes and settings) in a file on your iPhone. The file is protected by iOS data protection. If you turn on the app lock, Peony asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode before showing it.

Predictions, charts and insights are calculated on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere to produce them.

Test strip photos

If you use the LH strip reader, Peony uses your camera or photo library only when you ask it to. The photo is analysed on your iPhone. Peony keeps a small cropped image of the strip with that day's log and deletes it if you change the reading, clear the day or erase your data. Photos are never uploaded.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you allow it, Peony reads and writes the cycle data you choose (such as periods, spotting, sexual activity, basal body temperature, cervical mucus and test results) and can read sleeping wrist temperature. This exchange happens between apps on your iPhone. Peony only replaces Health entries it wrote itself.

Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with anyone. You can change or withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing › Apps › Peony.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone with iOS notifications. No notification content leaves your device.

Purchases

Peony Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your name, card details or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple, on your iPhone, whether you have an active purchase. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Exports and backups

You can export a backup file or a spreadsheet. These are created only when you ask, and you choose where they go (for example Files, AirDrop or email). Once a file leaves Peony, it is handled by whatever app or service you send it to.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, iOS includes Peony's data in that device backup, as it does for other apps. That backup is managed by Apple and your device settings, not by us.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party code that sees your data.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data, including with data brokers, insurers or employers.

Because we don't hold your data, we can't hand it over to anyone, including in response to a legal request.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase all logged data in Peony, or delete the app. Data Peony wrote to Apple Health stays in Health until you delete it there.

Children

Peony is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Peony's predictions are estimates. They are not a method of contraception or a substitute for advice from a doctor.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date. If a future version adds a feature that sends data off your iPhone, it will be optional, explained in the app first, and described here before release.
